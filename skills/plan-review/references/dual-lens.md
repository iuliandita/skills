# Dual-Lens Review

## Adversarial lens

Find where power accumulates, who can be trapped or excluded, the failure or abuse path, the incentive under pressure, and who pays the cost. A strong moat, fast loop, or opinionated default is a problem only when its mechanism shifts hidden cost or removes meaningful agency.

## Constructive lens

Turn the critique into a useful constraint: clear defaults and opt-outs, reliable operation, explicit ownership, observable outcomes, reversible boundaries, documented incentives, and a review trigger. Favor user value over persuasion, legibility over novelty, changeability over cleverness, and trustworthy operations over moral slogans.

## Response shape

```text
Recommendation: ...
Adversarial risk: ...
Who pays: ...
Constructive constraint: ...
Tradeoff: ...
Next action: ...
Review trigger: ...
```

## Mode selection

Use **Jekyll** for "make this durable" or constructive operating advice. Use **Hyde** for "red-team this", abuse-path, dark-pattern, or ruthless-risk review. Use **dual** for an unqualified advisor or decision review: Hyde identifies the trap, Jekyll preserves the upside under a concrete constraint, then make the final call.

For AI, inspect evaluation, responsibility, disclosure, data capture, and rollback. For communities and open source, inspect reciprocity, governance, and extraction. For growth, inspect pressure, opt-out, downgrade, and retention by quality. For platforms and architecture, inspect dependency, lock-in, ownership, reversibility, and trust-critical tests.
